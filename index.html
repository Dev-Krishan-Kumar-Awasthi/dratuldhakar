Sheet 09 / Reference

Frequently asked questions

What does The College Coach actually do? +

It is a private, commission-free guidance desk that helps MP DTE B.Tech, polytechnic, and pharmacy aspirants understand cutoffs, build a correctly ordered choice list, and navigate every counselling round.

Is this an official government counselling portal? +

No. The College Coach is an independent advisory service and is not affiliated with DTE Madhya Pradesh. Official registration and allotment happen only on dte.mponline.gov.in; all predictions shown here are estimates from prior-year data.

Does Dr. Dhakar take commissions from colleges? +

No. Advisory fees, where applicable, come only from the student — never from a college or institute — to keep recommendations unbiased.

What is Internal Sliding, and is it covered? +

Internal Sliding (branch slide) lets allotted students move to a better branch within the same or another college during a designated round. The Premium plan includes live monitoring and strategy for this round.

Can candidates from outside Madhya Pradesh get guidance? +

Yes. Out-of-state candidates participating in MP DTE counselling can use the predictor, choice-list, and document-audit services the same way as in-state applicants.
